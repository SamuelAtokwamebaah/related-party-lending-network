WHAT THESE DOCUMENTS DO AND DO NOT SETTLE

They establish, in the Registrar's own words, that a voluntary strike-off of this
company was begun, was suspended because an objection was received, and
completed in dissolution on 22 October 2019.

They do not name the objector. They do not name any individual. They do not
mention Ghana, First African Savings and Loans, or any sum owed. Nothing here
identifies this company as the affiliate the Bank of Ghana describes, and RR007
remains undecided.
