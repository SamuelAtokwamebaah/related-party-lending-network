WHAT THIS DOCUMENT DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE

It states, in the court's own voice and as undisputed, that GN Savings and Loans is a
member of Groupe Nduom. The introduction on pages 1 to 2 dates that membership: GN
"are/were" a member "until" it went into receivership. The Bank of Ghana notice already
places GN's related parties in "the Groupe Ndoum network of businesses" but never states
GN's own membership in terms. This document does.

It confirms, from a second source, that Gold Coast Advisors was formerly Ghana Growth
Fund Company Ltd, the name change the Bank of Ghana and SEC notices already record.

Two passages are transcribed here and deliberately not admitted as edges:

- "an affiliate company of the 3rd respondent" (page 13) is the trial judge's summary of
  the applicants' case, not a finding of the Court of Appeal.
- The assignment of receivables (pages 14 to 15) is evidence the court weighed on the
  value of GN's assets, which is the solvency question under appeal. It also runs into
  GN, not out of it, so it is not insider lending in the sense this project models.
